Wikipedia, but it learns what you like.

Qwikipedia shows you a personalised feed of Wikipedia articles that adapts to your interests as you interact with it. The algorithm runs entirely on your device, nothing you read is ever sent to a server.

Inspired by Xikipedia - a clever project by rebane2001. I loved the idea so I built Qwikipedia - thedevricardo: my own version with a cleaner design, proper accounts with full cloud sync, multi-language support, AI-enhanced search and videos, and a few extra features I thought were missing.

Your Data

Your recommendation history and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. If you sign in, your preferences, likes, saved articles, interests, topic weights, AI toggle, feed history, and stats are synced to your Supabase account and restored on other devices.

About

Qwikipedia shows you a personalised feed of Wikipedia articles. The recommendation algorithm runs locally - no data about what you read is sent to any server. Content is sourced from the Wikimedia REST API and the MediaWiki Action API, both freely available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Qwikipedia is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Wikimedia Foundation, Wikipedia, or Xikipedia.
