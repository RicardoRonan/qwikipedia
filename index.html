Wikipedia, but it learns what you like.

Qwikipedia shows you a personalised feed of Wikipedia articles that adapts to your interests as you interact with it. The algorithm runs entirely on your device, nothing you read is ever sent to a server.

Inspired by Xikipedia — a clever project by rebane2001. I loved the idea and built Qwikipedia: my own version with a cleaner design, proper accounts, multi-language support, and a few extra features I thought were missing.

Your Data

Your recommendation history and preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage. No article reading data is sent anywhere unless you are signed in, in which case only your theme and text size preferences are synced.

About

Qwikipedia shows you a personalised feed of Wikipedia articles. The recommendation algorithm runs locally — no data about what you read is sent to any server. Content is sourced from the Wikimedia REST API and the MediaWiki Action API, both freely available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
